# PLAYORA reusable social kit

The original landscape background remains `../playora-social-characters.png` (1672 × 941). Three independent compositions preserve its forest-green fantasy direction: `playora-square.png` (1254 × 1254), `playora-portrait.png` (1122 × 1402, approximately 4:5), and `playora-story.png` (941 × 1672, approximately 9:16). These are native generated PNG outputs, not enlarged 4K assets. All show generated interpretations of Miya (MLBB), Illidan (WoW) and Ahri (LoL). They have no embedded text. Exact prompts are in `PROMPTS.md`.

`landscape-editable.svg`, `square-editable.svg`, `portrait-editable.svg` and `story-editable.svg` reference those backgrounds and contain the application’s actual vector logo plus editable campaign copy. Keep this directory alongside its parent landscape master when opening an SVG in a vector editor. Fonts in campaign copy are editable; the wordmark itself uses vector paths. The editable overlays are not flattened raster exports. Head silhouettes, logo separation and central negative space were checked; inspect the target service’s own crop preview before publishing. No universal platform-safe crop or current platform upload requirement is claimed.

`avatar.svg` and its 512 × 512 `avatar.png` browser-rendered vector export use only the P symbol, with generous circular-crop padding on a green square. The website’s actual navigation wordmark, light/mono variants and favicon remain in `frontend/public/brand/`; do not use a detailed character scene at favicon size.

The optimized landscape `frontend/public/brand/playora-share.jpg` is 1200 × 675 and bounded to 500 KiB. It is a proportional JPEG export, not a repaint, and is used in homepage share metadata only. Browsers do not load it as ordinary page artwork. Set optional frontend `PUBLIC_SITE_ORIGIN` to the final HTTPS origin to enable canonical/share URLs; absent or malformed configuration emits no invented URL. Private account pages do not include customer details in share tags. Metadata follows the [Open Graph protocol](https://ogp.me/), checked 13 September 2026, including type, absolute canonical/image URL, image dimensions and descriptive alt text.

The owner permits changing character selection when another choice better fits the green theme. Current application motion uses Iori/Illidan/Ahri. Miya is used in this social kit and remains an optional application portrait. The original abstract aurora and all prior logo/background masters remain intact. Generated depictions do not establish official licensing; the already-planned publication-rights review remains in `final-launch.md`. No social publishing or R10/R11 work is performed here.
