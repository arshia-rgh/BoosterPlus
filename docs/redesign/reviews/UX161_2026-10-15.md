## Final scope and observations

The final login treatment has the top navbar and one large artwork scene containing the sign-in card. The separate footer and site ambient layer are absent in all nine final captures, including the representative MFA route after primary identity verification. Customer/admin/booster identity copy, invitation language and MFA requirements remain readable. No authentication behavior was changed by the reviewer.

The wider composition keeps the cloaked character and illuminated gate visible on desktop and phones. The card sits over the right side of the scene without hiding either subject; the mobile artwork band displays both above the story and form. The official PLAYORA logo remains registered over the gate lintel and reads as a mounted plaque. It remains recognizable on the phone crop. The final artwork, story and opaque form panel retain clear separation and readable text. No further material-treatment correction is required for this preview.

Rain and the localized lightning stay behind the controls and have `pointer-events: none`. Rendered peak captures show the restrained bolt and ambient glow without obscuring the form or subjects. Computed animations use the reviewed single 22-second lightning cycle, with peak bolt/glow opacities 0.55/0.12. Peak views were captured by positioning the native animation timeline; no claim of watching an entire natural 22-second cycle is made. The earlier independent composition round measured actual rain transform progression. Final Chromium desktop/phone and WebKit phone reduced-motion checks hide all three decorative effects and leave zero associated animations.

## Review round trips

The initial phone/tablet story crossed a bright cloud/castle region. The implementer identified the weak contrast and the reviewer confirmed it in retained renders. The final dedicated art band moves story text below the subjects onto the dark scene, resolving this condition. The owner's later wider-art request also resolved subject cropping/overlap observed in earlier compositions. Final inspected desktop, narrow-desktop, tablet and phone captures show the character and gate unobscured.

A separate built-browser check found inline rain variables blocked by CSP; the implementer replaced them with stylesheet rules. This review retains the local rendered result and does not substitute for that separate built-browser qualification. No remaining actionable design finding is open.
