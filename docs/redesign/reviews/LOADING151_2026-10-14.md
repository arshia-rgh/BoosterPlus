# Loading151 scoped design review

14 October 2026. Base: `c927d4a9f66075ca78f0a81a04e7add8f42188ee`.

The owner requested a polished loading treatment in place of visible sign-in/access checking text during navigation. This review covers the shared branded loading component, its full workspace and compact form presentations, and replacements in customer/admin/booster identity, custom/fixed order submission, shop purchase, resale purchase, intake image, article/product/inventory/submission editors and assignment/order access refreshes. It does not change authentication, session storage or financial behavior.

## Acceptance

- Green PLAYORA symbol, restrained orbit/pulse, no fake percentage or added minimum wait.
- Accessible loading status with decorative elements hidden from assistive technology and keyboard focus.
- Automatic reduced-motion response, without introducing a motion setting.
- Pending access never exposes protected content. Failed access retains readable errors/retry; account purchase does not flash a signed-out action before a successful session response.
- Responsive within existing content, without overlaying navigation or creating horizontal overflow.

## Independent review

Reviewer: `/root/loading151_senior_review`, separate senior UI/UX designer. Preview: isolated `http://127.0.0.1:4181`, using production Identity and frame components with synthetic responses. Round 1 initially found the preview middleware returning the fallback page instead of delaying the API. The fixture middleware was moved before Vite handling and the reviewer reloaded and verified the intended states. This was a review-fixture problem, not an application defect.

Final verdict: **PASS — no remaining actionable findings in the loading UI scope.**

The reviewer independently inspected live 1280 px admin, 768 px booster/tablet and admin failure, 320 px customer/compact purchase states, and captured 390 px admin/desktop customer frames. The orbit visibly advanced, pulse and contrast fit the theme, no narrow overflow was observed, and connection errors and keyboard-reachable retry remained clear. Reviewed Chromium and WebKit evidence covers eight passing scenarios including reduced motion, axe, authorization withholding, failure and retry to signed-out.

## Evidence and limits

Private evidence: `.cache/loading151/browser-chromium`, `browser-webkit`, corresponding logs and `senior-review.json`. The focused unit run passed 29 cases; type checking passed. The normal frontend/repository commit hook remains required separately. No full database/browser/production suite is claimed.

Synthetic sessions only: no real account, provider purchase, outgoing funds or email was used. Physical devices and screen-reader audio were not tested. The finding-free review does not guarantee absence of undiscovered issues.
