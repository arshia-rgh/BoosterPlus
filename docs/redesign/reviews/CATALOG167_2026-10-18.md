## Scope

## Acceptance criteria

- Three clearly labeled single-choice win-rate options and three delivery options replace free-form MLBB ranges. Surcharge labels are visible near the options; one concise note explains the common base. Existing provisional/final-quote and delivery-start conditions remain understandable.
- The estimate and breakdown clearly distinguish the base boost price from each independent add-on. No stale total is presented as current while a new estimate is pending or invalid. Legacy rank, win-rate and delivery records remain readable.
- Hero selection retains the linked-role behavior and makes the selected count and applicable surcharge understandable. Empty or above-five selections must follow a defined catalog rule, not silently imply an invented surcharge.
- Seller intake groups skins by category with quantity and individual names. Quantity/name mismatches, missing categories and invalid counts are associated with the exact field; progression focuses the first invalid field. A seller with no skins can represent that honestly.
- Public cards show compact labeled Heroes and Skins facts plus a brief authored reason to buy. These additions must not reintroduce a wall of text or displace the buyer total/action. Full named skin groups belong in account details, staff preview and accepted-record presentation where applicable.
- Missing collection information in legacy listings is distinguishable from a confirmed zero. Long names, titles, pitch text and collections wrap without horizontal page overflow or indistinguishable alignment.
- Private seller data does not become public without normal admin selection/review. Existing public records and accepted orders retain their authored content.
- Desktop, tablet and genuinely narrow phone renders, keyboard/focus, validation, loading/failure and relevant signed-in/out states are checked against the changed source. The final verdict follows correction/review rounds until no scoped actionable findings remain.
