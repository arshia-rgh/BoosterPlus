## Design judgment

Glacier remains my recommended broad-use direction: neutral slate carries dense operational content well, ice-blue secondary details are distinct from green primary actions, and artwork keeps its natural hues. Iris is a coherent more expressive gaming alternative. Champagne is a coherent restrained marketplace alternative; semantic warning/error samples remain separately identified with text/icons. These are proposals for owner selection, not authority to change the live theme.
