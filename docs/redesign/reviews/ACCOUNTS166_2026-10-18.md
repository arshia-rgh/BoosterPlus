# ACCOUNTS166 scoped senior UI/UX review

Date: 18 October 2026. Reviewer: independent agent `accounts166_senior_design`.

## Scope and limits

This review covers the public account marketplace cards, account detail and purchase-review presentation, and affected shared gallery/private staff listing-preview components. The wider UX165 flow audit remains paused. Existing products, authored legal terms, accepted agreements, commissions, checkout/authentication protections and green/champagne visual direction must remain unchanged.

No live session, order, message, invoice, publication or funds action is part of this review. Browser qualification uses isolated synthetic fixtures; rendered fixture checks do not establish persisted backend journey coverage. The full gate is not requested.

## Acceptance criteria

- Each card makes the account title, labeled rank/region, a small set of relevant attributes and buyer total clear on first scan. The 5% commission inclusion stays explicit. There is one primary details action and a truthful cover/no-image state.
- The detail screen leads with the account identity, gallery, concise facts and price/purchase action. Full authored description and contractual terms remain accessible in clearly named sections, without placing a wall of legal text before the principal next step.
- The purchase review retains an exact account/price summary, complete available terms, explicit consent and clear reservation/payment expectations, without duplicating the complete detail page inside itself.
- Gallery controls, native enlarged-image dialog, focus return, loading/failure/empty states, unavailable accounts and signed-in/out review states remain usable.
- Desktop, tablet and actual 320/390-pixel widths preserve readable hierarchy without clipping or horizontal page overflow. Long titles, attribute values and terms are included in the check.
- Shared staff previews reflect the public presentation while remaining plainly private; temporary grants and expiry behavior are preserved.

## Initial source review

The delivered presentation combines rank and region into prose, repeats title/price/detail content, places purchase progression after the complete contractual text, and styles card `h2` elements even though embedded marketplace cards use `h3`. No-image covers occupy substantial card space. These are source observations supporting the scoped redesign, not rendered qualification findings.
