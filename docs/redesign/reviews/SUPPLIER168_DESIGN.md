## Scope and boundaries

The current owner direction authorizes Nexus and Vendoria integration, test environments first: import their complete catalogs privately; supplier cost plus a configurable 15% default markup and explicit administrator price overrides; retain PLAYORA-authored/manual products; take the customer's PLAYORA payment before buying from a supplier. ## Recommended screen structure

| Surface | Content order and action hierarchy |
| --- | --- |
| Private shop catalog | Page title; compact actions for **Import supplier catalog**, **New manual product**, and **Delivery queue**; filters; result count and rows. Each row shows product/game, source, delivery method, public state, price rule, availability and one named edit action. Supplier imports must not displace manual creation. |
| Supplier import | Supplier and explicit environment; last successful catalog update; import scope; preview counts; **Import as private drafts**. Completion shows added, updated, unchanged, and needs-review counts with links to their filtered rows. Progress and partial failure remain visible and recoverable. | Imported product record | Persistent product identity and source/environment; existing local sections, with a clear supplier/pricing section. Separate received supplier facts from PLAYORA-authored presentation and overrides. Show current published price beside the private proposed price when they differ. |
| Pricing | Read-only supplier unit cost and currency, cost freshness, **Use markup** / **Set fixed selling price**, a normal decimal amount field, and calculated customer unit price. Put the resulting price next to the control that changes it. A fixed-price override must look different from an inherited rule. |
| Public shop | Retain current game shell; delivery groups ahead of the result grid; search plus populated game/region/category choices where applicable; result count and active filters. Cards lead with title, selected compatibility, delivery method/estimate, customer price, availability and one detail action. |
| Product and purchase review | Product/variant compatibility and delivery method above progression. Collect only that method's required inputs. Review repeats exact variant, quantity, masked recipient, customer total, required participation and accepted terms. Preserve the order/payment handoff with one main action. |
| Customer delivery | Keep persistent order identity. Lead with current delivery state, who acts next and the specific action. Then show accepted product/estimate, current request or delivered result, local support, and complete accepted terms in disclosures. |
| Admin delivery | Keep payment, supplier submission and delivery outcome distinct. Lead with actionable exceptions and current actor; retain supplier reference, last confirmed update and safe recovery context. The existing manual claim/review path remains available for manual products. |

Public groups may use the requested **ID & automatic delivery** and **Account sign-in · 24–72h** labels, but each card must still name its actual method: **Player ID**, **Digital code**, **Account sign-in**, or **PLAYORA staff delivery**. Keep manual products discoverable under All and an appropriate staff/manual filter or group. A digital code must not be described as a player-ID top-up, and “automatic” must not be presented as a delivery-time guarantee. Product-specific approved estimates govern where they differ.
