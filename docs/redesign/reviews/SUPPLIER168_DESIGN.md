## Scope and boundaries

The current owner direction authorizes Nexus and Vendoria integration, test environments first: import their complete catalogs privately; supplier cost plus a configurable 15% default markup and explicit administrator price overrides; retain PLAYORA-authored/manual products; take the customer's PLAYORA payment before buying from a supplier. Discovery distinguishes ID/automatic delivery from account-sign-in delivery estimated at 24–72 hours, with useful game, region and category filters. Provider capabilities can include delivered codes and interactive code, Google prompt, game-exit and image requests. Supplier name alone cannot determine the delivery method or support an instant-delivery claim.

Affected surfaces are public shop browse/product/purchase review within the existing game shell, the private product list/import/pricing/editor/preview, the shop delivery queue, and the shop portion of customer/admin order records. Review of shared components is limited to those affected contexts. Green/champagne presentation, separate identities/MFA, accepted order records, and existing order-local payment/help/message destinations remain. The paused UX165 audit is not resumed. No credentials, live services, customer mutations, messages, purchases or funds were used in this review. Untracked prototype/cameo files were not changed.

Sources read: `frontend/app/shop/{model,public,list,editor,fields,preview,queue,delivery,shop.css}`, the shop route wrappers, game shell, order workspace integration points, the design change gate, shop/news addendum, and prior scoped review records. The supplier API characteristics above come from the task handoff; this reviewer has not independently certified provider contracts or their live behavior.

## Recommended screen structure

| Surface | Content order and action hierarchy |
| --- | --- |
| Private shop catalog | Page title; compact actions for **Import supplier catalog**, **New manual product**, and **Delivery queue**; filters; result count and rows. Each row shows product/game, source, delivery method, public state, price rule, availability and one named edit action. Supplier imports must not displace manual creation. |
| Supplier import | Supplier and explicit environment; last successful catalog update; import scope; preview counts; **Import as private drafts**. Completion shows added, updated, unchanged, and needs-review counts with links to their filtered rows. Progress and partial failure remain visible and recoverable. Publication is a separate reviewed operation. |
| Imported product record | Persistent product identity and source/environment; existing local sections, with a clear supplier/pricing section. Separate received supplier facts from PLAYORA-authored presentation and overrides. Show current published price beside the private proposed price when they differ. |
| Pricing | Read-only supplier unit cost and currency, cost freshness, **Use markup** / **Set fixed selling price**, a normal decimal amount field, and calculated customer unit price. Put the resulting price next to the control that changes it. A fixed-price override must look different from an inherited rule. |
| Public shop | Retain current game shell; delivery groups ahead of the result grid; search plus populated game/region/category choices where applicable; result count and active filters. Cards lead with title, selected compatibility, delivery method/estimate, customer price, availability and one detail action. |
| Product and purchase review | Product/variant compatibility and delivery method above progression. Collect only that method's required inputs. Review repeats exact variant, quantity, masked recipient, customer total, required participation and accepted terms. Preserve the order/payment handoff with one main action. |
| Customer delivery | Keep persistent order identity. Lead with current delivery state, who acts next and the specific action. Then show accepted product/estimate, current request or delivered result, local support, and complete accepted terms in disclosures. |
| Admin delivery | Keep payment, supplier submission and delivery outcome distinct. Lead with actionable exceptions and current actor; retain supplier reference, last confirmed update and safe recovery context. The existing manual claim/review path remains available for manual products. |

Public groups may use the requested **ID & automatic delivery** and **Account sign-in · 24–72h** labels, but each card must still name its actual method: **Player ID**, **Digital code**, **Account sign-in**, or **PLAYORA staff delivery**. Keep manual products discoverable under All and an appropriate staff/manual filter or group. A digital code must not be described as a player-ID top-up, and “automatic” must not be presented as a delivery-time guarantee. Product-specific approved estimates govern where they differ.
