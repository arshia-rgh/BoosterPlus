## Owner decisions incorporated

The owner requested worldwide positioning without publishing the business/server location; 24/7 online support and fulfillment across boosting, accounts, shop and other offered services; package/order-specific WR and other boosting conditions; a 100% refund when agreed order conditions are not delivered; and a 100% account refund when review establishes that a reclaim, unusable account, ban or similar failure was not caused by the buyer. Per-game/service help is included. These replace the earlier blank hours/WR/refund questions.

The owner also authorized filling the remaining drafting choices using research. Customer-facing documents now have complete prose rather than dozens of owner placeholders. We have not invented a legal entity, jurisdiction, registration, address, executed transfer agreement, operational test result or provider capability.

## How the full refund is defined

The affected order receives its full purchase amount, not just the unfinished portion. No deduction for partial work, internal administration or PLAYORA's processing/refund fees. The drafted default is the original asset and original units allocated to the order, with PLAYORA covering the outgoing fee. Other currency/network arrangements require explicit agreement. Third-party wallet fees paid separately and unrelated subsequent game spending are distinguished from the order price. Previous partial refunds count toward the same 100% obligation; duplicate/excess payments remain separate obligations.

Account protection expressly includes seller reclamation, missing agreed access, material misdescription, undisclosed earlier misconduct and non-buyer-caused publisher restrictions, including a transfer-related ban. It has no newly invented 7/14/30-day cutoff, and normal use or security changes do not automatically void it. Evidence must connect buyer conduct to the reported damage. No “seller did not repay us” defense against an eligible buyer refund is proposed.

This is a broad commercial commitment. Cases can arise after order completion; the application, review staffing and refund reserve must support it before the wording goes live. It is not a claim that every reported account problem has already been verified or must bypass fraud review.

## Account damage compensation

The owner's additional instruction covers harm caused by PLAYORA staff or boosters, including damaging losses or a lower WR. The Terms and help now distinguish monetary compensation for verified account harm from the existing full affected-order refund. A missing purchased WR guarantee does not prevent a damage claim, and reaching the target rank does not automatically defeat it. Ordinary match variance is distinguished from damage using starting-state, match and order evidence.

Compensation is assessed individually, with written reasons, supported recovery/replacement costs and lasting harm considered. No arbitrary payout tariff, liability cap or automatic promise of a win in every match is invented. Restoration requires consent, outgoing fees are borne by PLAYORA, and an undisputed refund cannot be held until a damage settlement is accepted. Compensation is not implemented by editing these drafts; separate approval, accounting and payment qualification remain necessary.

The owner also requested reassuring wording that harm/non-delivery is almost nonexistent. The drafts express the commitment to correct delivery and account protection, without asserting an unmeasured near-zero incident rate or an “almost always” success record. A future performance claim needs verified outcomes, a defined sample/period and clear incident definitions; neither policy wording nor automated tests establish real customer success rates.

## Publication facts that research cannot replace

Public copy is location-neutral and presents worldwide service, as requested. Nevertheless, some customer markets require the trader/controller's real identity and contact/address disclosures. For example, the [European Commission's marketplace guidance](https://commission.europa.eu/topics/consumers/consumer-rights-and-complaints/enforcement-consumer-protection/coordinated-actions/market-places-and-digital-services_en) describes identity/geographical-address disclosures, and the [ICO transparency checklist](https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/uk-gdpr-guidance-and-resources/individual-rights/individual-rights/right-to-be-informed/) addresses the responsible organization and international processing.

Calling the business global does not resolve these requirements. The legal operator and relevant disclosure/transfer obligations remain an explicit pre-publication review item; they are not fabricated or waived. No personal/home address is requested for a public chat, no server location is included in the customer drafts, and no arbitrary governing law or foreign-court restriction is invented.

A worldwide headline also cannot override a game's regions or a payment provider's restrictions. The drafts therefore qualify actual availability and retain the provider-verification explanation. Publishing guarantees requires actual coverage and operating procedures, not merely a terms checkbox.
