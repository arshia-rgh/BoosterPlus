# PLAYORA game and service help

**Customer-copy draft,4 October 2026. Publish only on the corresponding supported game/service pages after checking the live offer fields.** No rank table, patch detail, current product availability or unverified help URL is invented here.

## Shared introduction

Find help for your game by opening its game page and choosing Boosting, Accounts, Shop or the available information section. Read the selected offer's details and FAQs before ordering. Support and order fulfillment operate 24/7; if an answer is missing, contact us through your account or at support@playoragames.com.

## Questions for every game

**How do I know exactly what I am buying?**

Your package or accepted custom quote defines the game, region/server, requirements, price and delivery conditions. Review it before paying. An estimated price is not a final custom quote, and an estimated time is not a guaranteed deadline.

**Is a particular win rate guaranteed?**

Only when that condition is included in the package or accepted order. Its match scope and measurement must be stated there. PLAYORA has no single global WR promise. Ask support to clarify anything ambiguous before acceptance.

**What if an agreed condition is not delivered?**

You receive a 100% refund of the affected order's purchase amount under our Terms. Partial progress does not reduce this promise. You do not have to accept store credit, a replacement or another attempt instead of an eligible full refund.

**Does24/7 mean instant completion?**

Our teams provide support and process orders around the clock. The actual service still takes the time described in your order and may require payment confirmation, a scheduled session or information from you. Check the order for progress and contact support at any time.

**Where do I send game access details?**

Use the designated private credential or delivery screen. Do not put passwords in ordinary chat or screenshots. Staff do not need your PLAYORA login code, personal email password, wallet private key or seed phrase.

**How do account problems get resolved?**

Report missing access, misdescription, reclamation, restrictions or a ban in the order conversation or by email. Include relevant dates and notices with unrelated personal details hidden. If review confirms you did not cause the problem, our 100% account-refund protection applies. Normal play or following agreed security steps does not automatically cancel protection.

**Are account sharing, buying or boosting approved by the publisher?**

PLAYORA is independent. Publisher rules may restrict these activities and can lead to account restrictions. Read the game-specific information and official publisher rules before buying. That warning does not remove PLAYORA's expressly promised refund protection.

**Why does payment still say pending?**

A blockchain transfer and verified order payment are separate stages. We check provider evidence and confirmations. Do not pay again just because a callback is delayed; contact support with the order reference. Use only the asset/network and destination shown by checkout.

**How will my refund arrive?**

We verify an appropriate destination and normally refund the original asset and units allocated to the order, with PLAYORA covering the outgoing fee for a guaranteed full refund. We never force a wallet balance. Read the Terms for amount definitions, external wallet costs and processing targets.
