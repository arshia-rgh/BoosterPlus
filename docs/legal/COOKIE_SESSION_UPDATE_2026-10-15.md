# Customer session duration — cookie notice amendment

Prepared factual copy for the owner-requested session change. This is not a new published policy revision; preserve the existing approved policy and its historical agreements until normal authenticated publication.

Replace the combined session-cookie row in the Cookies notice with:

| Item | Purpose | Duration |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `__Host-bp-customer-session` | Keep you signed in to your customer account | Up to 90 days from sign-in. The server ends the session after 30 days without activity, at its absolute expiry, when you sign out or revoke it, or when an account-security change requires it. Existing sessions retain their earlier absolute expiry until you sign in again. |
| `__Host-bp-admin-session`, `__Host-bp-booster-session` | Sign in to the separate staff workspace | Up to 12 hours from sign-in, with a 30-minute inactivity limit. Revocation and account-security changes may end a session sooner. |

All other cookie/storage descriptions stay unchanged. Customer session duration does not remove verification required for sensitive actions. Staff still complete their authenticator at every sign-in.
