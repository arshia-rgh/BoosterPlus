## Worldwide service and support

The PLAYORA team offers game boosting, account buying/selling, approved in-game shop products and staff-managed game information. Support and fulfillment teams operate 24/7. Availability depends on the offering, game, region/server, stock and supported payment route; it does not promise immediate completion. Contact support@playoragames.com or use your private order conversation.

You must be at least 18 and legally able to enter the agreement. Use only accounts and payment methods you are authorized to use. PLAYORA sign-in uses Google or email. Customer, admin and booster identities are separate; staff sign-in requires an authenticator. Never share PLAYORA codes or wallet secrets.

## What you buy

A fixed package has a stated scope and price. A custom form gives an estimate; staff issue the final offer for you to accept before payment. Your agreement records the game, region/server, result, conditions, price and delivery details. Later catalog edits do not rewrite an accepted order.

Rank, win rate, hero/role restrictions and guaranteed timing are agreed per order. A request alone is not a guarantee. Use the protected access flow when playing on your account; playing together normally does not require your password. Shop products are manually delivered by staff, and account sales use private handover with disclosed access and restrictions.

## Account commission

For new account-sale agreements, buyers pay the account base price plus 5%, and sellers receive the agreed base minus 10%. The fees are calculated independently and rounded to USD cents, half upward. At a $100 base, buyer total is $105 and seller net is $90. The checkout and seller offer must show the breakdown before acceptance. A PLAYORA-funded discount does not reduce the seller's accepted net. These commissions do not apply to boosting or shop purchases and are not added retroactively to accepted orders.

Seller offers distinguish consignment from buy-first. Consignment depends on a verified buyer sale, acknowledged handover, the seven-day initial inspection period and resolution of relevant issues, followed by a 48-hour payout-initiation target with a verified destination. Buy-first targets initiation within 48 hours after verified agreed handover and destination, before resale; a later resale price cannot reduce the accepted seller payout. Exact crypto amounts, offer expiry, fees, withdrawal and dispute conditions are recorded in the offer. PLAYORA pays outgoing payout costs. No seller payment is required in advance to unlock a payout.

## Payment and protection

Pay only through the supported direct crypto checkout. The initial route is USDT on BNB Smart Chain (BEP-20). Follow the exact invoice amount, network, address and expiry. There is no user wallet, top-up or escrow service. Provider evidence must be verified before fulfillment. Provider checks or network delays do not simulate success; contact support rather than making a duplicate payment.

For a reclaimed, banned, restricted, unusable, materially misdescribed account or missing promised access, the same full refund applies when review confirms the buyer did not cause the issue. It includes the 5% buyer commission. Ordinary use, appropriate security changes, delivery completion, a seller payout or expiry of the initial inspection period does not end that protection.

Verified account damage caused by PLAYORA or its team receives fair monetary compensation separately from an eligible order refund. A lower WR can be reviewed without a purchased WR guarantee. Ordinary match variance is distinguished from verified harm using evidence. There is no automatic compensation cap at the order price or compulsory store credit.

A full refund normally returns the original asset and original units allocated to the order; PLAYORA covers outgoing refund costs. An alternative requires your agreement. Third-party wallet/exchange sending costs and unrelated later purchases are distinct, subject to any additional legal remedy. Previous refunds are accounted for without double payment.

Cancel for a full refund before actual fulfillment. Later change-of-mind deductions require a lawful pre-agreed basis for correctly supplied work and cannot reduce a protected failure-based refund. Mandatory withdrawal and consumer rights remain; general terms acceptance is not a substitute for separately required early-performance consent.

Publishers may prohibit boosting or account transfers. PLAYORA is independent and does not promise immunity from enforcement; that warning does not cancel the protection expressly promised here. Reviews and uploads must be genuine and respect privacy. Negative feedback is not grounds to withhold a refund or payout. Staff articles may become outdated and do not add an order guarantee.

The complete Terms of Service, Privacy and service-specific policies presented with your order explain these commitments in detail. They are versioned.
