# PLAYORA complaints and appeals

**Draft for owner review — 13 October 2026. Not published or effective.**

## Open a case

Contact the order or submission Help conversation, or email support@playoragames.com. Tell us the reference, issue, relevant dates and outcome you seek. You can contact support if you cannot sign in or an in-app reporting window has closed. Support is available 24/7; we do not charge a complaint-handling fee.

Buyers and sellers use separate private conversations. We share only the information needed to understand and answer the allegation, with sensitive material protected. Never post credentials, codes or private financial information as public evidence.

## How we review

We compare the accepted agreement with payment, delivery, access, progress, communication and relevant publisher evidence. We ask for information proportionate to the issue and retrieve records only PLAYORA holds ourselves. A problem appearing after completion is not automatically the customer's fault.

We aim to give a substantive assessment within 72 hours of receiving the necessary evidence. We explain any hold, what is outstanding and the next step, and provide updates at least every 48 hours while action is pending. A complicated case does not give us permission to ignore a shorter applicable legal deadline.

Our decision identifies the agreed condition, relevant evidence, reasoning and proposed remedy. A protected full refund includes the account buyer commission. Service-caused damage compensation is assessed separately; an undisputed refund is not withheld until compensation is settled. A buyer refund does not depend on first recovering funds from a seller.

## Ask for another review

If you disagree, reply to the case or email support with the decision you challenge and anything missed or misunderstood. An additional authorized reviewer examines the decision where available. If staffing prevents a different reviewer, we explain that and still reconsider the evidence; we do not pretend an independent reviewer exists.

We aim to complete this review within seven calendar days, with reasons and an updated timeframe for any delay. The current in-app appeal limits are 30 days per appeal and up to three appeals. The interface also normally allows disputes for 365 days after boost/shop completion; account listings state their own inspection/reporting period. These interface limits do not extinguish the accepted protections, remove a mandatory right or prevent contacting support about new evidence.

For moderation, privacy or seller-offer issues without an order appeal control, the support email provides the same route to request review. Privacy requests follow their applicable deadlines, including our one-calendar-month response target.

## Fair treatment and outside remedies

We do not retaliate for a genuine complaint, a negative review or the use of lawful dispute channels. Deliberately fabricated evidence, threats or account fraud may be investigated, but we preserve legitimate transaction obligations and mandatory rights. Security restrictions are not automatic forfeiture of paid orders or seller proceeds.

Nothing in this policy requires compulsory arbitration, waives a right that cannot lawfully be waived, or prevents contacting a competent consumer authority, data-protection authority, payment provider or court. No undisclosed country is designated as the only place you may seek a remedy. Inform us of a separate recovery already received so the same loss is not paid twice.
