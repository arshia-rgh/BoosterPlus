# PLAYORA refunds and account damage policy

**Draft for owner review — 13 October 2026. Not published or effective.**

## Our full refund promise

If PLAYORA fails an agreed deliverable or condition of your accepted order, you are entitled to **100% of the affected order's purchase payment back**. This includes an agreed rank, win rate, hero or role restriction, product, quantity, required access or guaranteed deadline. An explicitly labelled estimate is not a guaranteed deadline. Partial work does not reduce a full refund owed under this promise.

For an account purchase, the same protection applies when an account is reclaimed, banned, restricted, unusable, materially misdescribed or missing promised access and our evidence-based review confirms that the buyer did not cause the problem. Previous-owner actions and the transfer itself can be covered. We do not reject a claim simply because the account was played, secured following our instructions, marked delivered or previously paid out to its seller.

A replacement, repair, repeat service or extension may be offered with your consent. You do not have to accept it instead of an eligible monetary refund. We do not issue compulsory store credit or a PLAYORA wallet balance.

## What the refund includes

The refundable purchase amount is the payment actually allocated to the affected order, including charges collected by PLAYORA. For an account with a $100 base price and $5 buyer commission, a protected full refund covers the complete $105 purchase payment. PLAYORA does not retain that $5 fee, deduct its processing costs or charge an administration fee.

By default we return the original cryptocurrency and original number of units allocated to the order. PLAYORA covers the outgoing refund fee so it does not reduce that amount. A different supported asset, network or conversion basis requires your express agreement to the exact amount first. A display value in USD is not permission to recalculate a refund at a later exchange rate.

Previous partial refunds count toward the same order total; we do not pay twice for the same purchase amount. A PLAYORA-funded coupon reduces the amount you actually paid and therefore the purchase-refund amount. Duplicate or excess transfers are accounted for separately, not forfeited or converted into credit. Your wallet or exchange's separately charged sending costs, unrelated later game purchases and market-price changes are not included in the order purchase amount, without restricting any further remedy required by law.

## Cancellation and changes of mind

Before actual fulfillment starts, you can cancel for a full refund. Creating an invoice, allocating staff or conducting an internal review alone does not count as gameplay or product delivery. A completed account transfer is different from staff merely preparing it.

If you change your mind after correctly performed fulfillment has started, only a lawful charge for work actually supplied on a clearly disclosed, accepted basis may be considered. We explain the evidence and calculation. Without an applicable pre-agreed basis, we do not invent a deduction. These rules do not reduce a full refund owed for our failure or a protected account problem.

If we cannot fulfill the agreed order, we explain the issue and offer a full refund or an alternative you choose. We do not silently change the rank, product, deadline or fee. If necessary access or attendance is missing, we contact you to agree the next step; inactivity does not automatically turn undelivered work into completed work or create a no-show penalty.

Applicable statutory withdrawal and digital-service rights remain available. General terms acceptance does not record any separately required request for immediate performance or acknowledgement of the loss of a withdrawal right. An irreversible blockchain payment does not eliminate a legal refund obligation.

## Damage caused by our team

If PLAYORA, its staff or an assigned booster damages your account while providing the service, we provide fair monetary compensation for verified harm, separately from any full order refund owed. Relevant harm can include rank or win-rate damage, unauthorized asset spending, access loss or a restriction caused by our actions.

We compare the starting account state, agreed conditions, relevant match history and our team's actions. A lower win rate can be reviewed even if no separate WR guarantee was bought. A single loss or ordinary fluctuation is not automatically compensable harm; neither reaching the final rank nor the absence of a global WR promise automatically defeats a supported claim.

We assess reasonable evidenced recovery or replacement costs and lasting loss. We explain the proposed monetary amount; there is no fixed payment per lost game and no automatic cap at the order price. Restoration at our expense requires your consent and is not always technically possible. We do not force more gameplay or store credit instead of compensation. An undisputed refund is not held until a separate compensation amount is agreed, and we avoid paying twice for the same loss.

We confirm compensation amount, supported asset/network and destination before sending. PLAYORA covers the outgoing fee. Compensation and purchase refunds are recorded separately.

## Reporting and investigation

Use the order's Help conversation or email support@playoragames.com with the order reference, what happened, when it happened and the remedy you seek. Send relevant screenshots or publisher notices with unrelated private details removed. Do not send passwords, sign-in codes or wallet secrets. We retrieve records that only our team can access instead of requiring you to supply them.

We aim to provide a substantive assessment within 72 hours of receiving the necessary evidence and to initiate an approved refund within 48 hours of verifying a usable refund destination. These are service targets; any shorter applicable legal deadline prevails. A provider review or complex investigation receives an explanation and updates at least every 48 hours. We distinguish approval, submission and confirmed transfer.

For completed boosting and shop orders, the published payment policy provides a 365-day in-app reporting window, 30 days per in-app appeal and up to three appeals. Account listings use their accepted inspection/reporting window. These interface periods do not extinguish the protection in your agreement or applicable legal rights. If a button is unavailable, support can still review the claim.

Confirm the refund destination through authenticated support. An exchange's sending address is not necessarily a usable return address. We do not send to a new destination solely because a stranger requested it. If a safe handback of delivered assets is appropriate, support coordinates it; you are not required to recover an inaccessible account yourself.

## Decisions and appeals

We provide reasons and relevant evidence, distinguish a genuinely buyer-caused problem from our or a seller's failure, and offer a second review by another authorized reviewer where available. Request this in the case or by email. We aim to finish an appeal within seven calendar days and explain any delay.

A protected buyer refund is independent of recovery from a seller. Bad reviews, polite complaints and use of lawful dispute channels are not grounds to remove your protection. Deliberately fraudulent evidence is not covered. Mandatory consumer rights and access to an independent authority or court remain unaffected.
