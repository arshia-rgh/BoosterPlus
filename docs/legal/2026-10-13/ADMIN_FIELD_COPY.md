### Fees and deductions for either arrangement

The only routine seller commission is 10% of the agreed account base price, already included in the displayed net calculation. PLAYORA covers outgoing provider/network costs so the exact accepted net crypto amount is not reduced. No listing, offer, withdrawal or payout-unlock fee is payable in advance. A failed/cancelled sale is not automatically charged a successful-sale commission. Any disputed loss is reviewed separately with evidence and a right to respond; no arbitrary penalty, buyer-fee transfer or unagreed deduction applies. Existing accepted offers keep their own terms.

### Transfer and ownership requirements

The account, included access, verified attributes and disclosed restrictions are identified in this offer's account description. You confirm authority and current control, disclose relevant bans, warnings, recovery claims, transfer cooldowns, unpaid obligations and untransferable links, and stop concurrent sales. Provide only the account access needed through the protected handover process; never send PLAYORA codes, wallet secrets or unrelated personal email access in chat. Cooperate with the agreed transfer and do not recover or use the account after control passes without written permission. Public images must be accurate and authorized for publication. Staff verify control before resale or payout eligibility; submission approval alone is not that verification.

### Seller disputes and holds

PLAYORA investigates relevant payment, description, control and delivery disputes and gives you a chance to respond. A hold identifies the affected matter, receives updates at least every 48 hours and review at least every seven days. Buyer protection does not end after the seven-day inspection period or your payout. A later claim is not automatically your fault. If you fulfilled the agreement, a buyer- or PLAYORA-caused issue is not automatic grounds to reclaim your earned payout. Proven seller fraud, reclamation or material breach may lead to an evidenced repayment or direct-loss claim, with no double recovery or arbitrary penalty. Buyer refunds do not depend on recovering money from you. Request another review through your submission conversation or support@playoragames.com. Mandatory rights remain.

### Consignment withdrawal

You may decline an unaccepted offer or withdraw the submission without commission. After acceptance, request withdrawal through your submission conversation. If there is no reservation, paid sale or specific dispute hold, PLAYORA unpublishes the account and aims to return control securely within 48 hours, without a routine removal fee. A reservation or paid buyer order must first be resolved and payment reconciled; we explain the reason and next review time. Do not recover the account independently while another person may have paid for it. No completed sale means no sale proceeds, unless PLAYORA separately agrees to buy the account. Any reprice or new arrangement requires fresh acceptance.

### Buy-first withdrawal

You may decline an unaccepted offer without commission. Before verified transfer and payment, ask support to cancel and coordinate return of control; no successful-sale commission applies to a cancelled purchase. If a transfer is already submitted or its outcome is uncertain, reconciliation must finish before any reversal. Once PLAYORA completes the purchase, you cannot reclaim the account unilaterally. A reversal requires written agreement and a coordinated return of both account and payment. Your accepted net purchase payment does not depend on resale or become lower because PLAYORA later discounts the account. Statutory rights and evidenced breach remedies remain available.

## Buyer listing settings

Use the actual inspected game, server/region, assets, skin types/counts/names, cover image, included access, history and restrictions. Proposed ordinary handover range: **1–24 elapsed hours** after verified payment and complete handover prerequisites, expressly an estimate unless the listing guarantees a deadline. Do not use this range for an account that staff cannot actually hand over within it. Set `dispute_hours=168` for the seven-day inspection period; this does not limit later protected support claims.

The current single `amount_minor` field is the charged amount. It is **not** a new base-price field and does not automatically add 5%. Do not type a base and assume commission is added. ### Buyer transfer requirements

After verified payment and complete prerequisites, PLAYORA coordinates private handover of the access expressly included in the listing. Check login, region/server, advertised assets and required recovery controls, then perform the agreed password/security changes. Tell support immediately if anything is missing. Never put passwords, PLAYORA codes or wallet secrets in ordinary chat. Do not acknowledge full delivery before checking the promised access. Publisher transfer restrictions or cooldowns must be disclosed before purchase; staff cannot silently replace missing access with a promise of later delivery. The stated delivery range is an estimate unless expressly guaranteed.

### Buyer dispute terms

If the account is reclaimed, banned, restricted, unusable, materially misdescribed or missing promised access, and review confirms you did not cause the problem, PLAYORA refunds 100% of your purchase payment, including the 5% buyer commission. Previous-owner misconduct and transfer-caused restrictions can be covered. Ordinary play or following agreed security instructions does not cancel protection. Any alleged buyer fault must be connected by evidence to this problem. The seven-day in-app inspection period runs after acknowledged delivery; completion, expiry of that period and seller payout do not end your underlying protection. Contact support@playoragames.com if the reporting control is unavailable. A replacement is optional when a refund is due. Refund timing, separate damage compensation and appeals follow the accepted PLAYORA policies.

### Delivery acknowledgement terms

Confirm delivery only after checking that the account and promised login/recovery access match the accepted listing. Your acknowledgement starts the seven-day initial inspection period used for ordinary consignment payout timing. It does not confirm facts you could not inspect, waive hidden defects, make you responsible for prior-owner conduct, or end protected refund and damage claims. Report a problem promptly so evidence can be preserved. Staff must investigate missing acknowledgement, not enter it on your behalf without a valid authorized basis. Do not send credentials or wallet secrets with this acknowledgement.

## Shop policy fields

Proposed starting settings for a genuinely ready manual product: `delivery_min_hours=1`, `delivery_max_hours=24`, `reservation_minutes=30`, `evidence_required=true`, `review_enabled=true`. Actual provider expiry can be shorter. Staff must validate product compatibility, capacity and stock before choosing these values or publishing an item.

### Shop delivery terms

PLAYORA staff manually deliver the listed item and quantity to the confirmed compatible recipient after verified payment and complete required details. The stated range is an estimate in elapsed hours from those prerequisites unless this offer expressly guarantees a deadline. No product, platform, region or recipient substitution is made without agreement. Staff record delivery evidence and explain delays or missing information in the order. Payment confirmation alone is not delivery evidence. Do not send unrelated credentials or wallet secrets.

### Shop cancellation terms

Cancel for a full refund before actual fulfillment begins. After correctly performed work starts, only a lawful, clearly pre-agreed charge for work actually supplied may apply to a change-of-mind cancellation; we do not invent a deduction. This never reduces a full refund owed for failure of an accepted condition, an unusable or incorrect product, or a mandatory legal right. A paid but unavailable item is resolved by a full refund or an alternative you freely accept.

### Shop refund terms

If PLAYORA fails an agreed item, quantity, compatibility, access or guaranteed delivery condition, we refund 100% of the affected order purchase payment, even if part was delivered. PLAYORA covers the outgoing refund fee. By default the refund uses the original crypto asset and original units allocated to the order; a different basis needs your express agreement. We investigate wrong-recipient information on evidence. Verified damage caused by our delivery is assessed for separate fair monetary compensation. Contact the order's Help conversation or support@playoragames.com; an interface reporting limit does not erase accepted protections.

### Shop fees

The confirmed shop price includes PLAYORA's stated fulfillment charges. Account-sale buyer/seller commissions do not apply. PLAYORA pays its provider processing costs and outgoing protected-refund costs; separately charged wallet/exchange sending fees remain your responsibility. No undisclosed delivery, cancellation or administration fee is added after acceptance.

### Shop review terms

Reviews are optional and must reflect a real experience. Positive and negative feedback follow the same rules. Do not expose private information, credentials or payment details. PLAYORA may moderate threats, unlawful material, fabricated reviews, impersonation or spam, with reasons and a review route. We do not make refunds or support conditional on positive feedback or deletion of criticism. Public review permission is not consent to advertise with private messages or proof.
