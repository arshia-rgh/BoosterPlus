# PLAYORA complete policy draft package

**Prepared 13 October 2026 for owner review. Unpublished; no live terms, fees or settings changed.**

This package fills the policy areas identified in the site review, preserves the existing full-refund and account-damage commitments, and adds the owner's account-sale commission rule. It is a new version; the earlier approved source files remain unchanged.

**For an account base price of $100: the buyer pays $105; the seller receives $90.** The buyer's 5% and seller's 10% are both calculated independently from the base. PLAYORA's gross commission is $15 before costs when both sides use the same base. A protected full buyer refund includes the $5 buyer fee. A later buy-first resale at a different price has a different margin and does not reprice the original seller's accepted payout.

## What this does not activate

The current application needs an explicit base/fee/total/net breakdown and immutable calculations before the new 10%/5% model is used. Existing account listing prices are single payable amounts; text alone does not add a fee. The two reviewed seller submissions still need individual accepted offers with real prices and exact net payouts.

Outgoing transfers and deletion remain disabled. Retention fields currently impose minimum waits before profile deletion, not the complete schedule described here. The operator/controller and any legally required disclosures, tax treatment and international transfer arrangements remain factual review items; worldwide wording cannot replace them. The drafts do not publish fictional products, stock, legal details, game guarantees or staffing capacity.

After review, approve a dated publication version and qualify the missing behavior before normal authenticated publication. Keep prior agreements and payment policy revision 3. No full application gate or deployment is needed for this documentation-only stage.
