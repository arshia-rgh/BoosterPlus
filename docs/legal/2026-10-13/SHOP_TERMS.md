# PLAYORA shop terms

**Draft for owner review — 13 October 2026. Not published or effective.**

## Products and compatibility

PLAYORA's shop offers the in-game products expressly listed for each supported game. Staff manually fulfill approved orders after verified payment. A product must identify its actual item or entitlement, quantity, game/platform, eligible region/server, recipient requirements, delivery method, price and delivery estimate or guarantee. If it is time-limited or has a use restriction, that condition must be visible before purchase.

Check compatibility carefully. A matching game name alone does not establish that an item works on every server or platform. Region and server details must describe the intended recipient. If the relevant product is unavailable or its conditions are unclear, contact support before paying.

We do not offer a site wallet or account top-up. Any in-game currency product must be an explicitly approved shop listing; a news article or picture does not create an offer. Account-sale commissions of 5% and 10% do not apply to shop purchases.

## Price, stock and payment

The product and checkout show the final purchase price and any conditions of a discount. We do not add an undisclosed service or delivery fee after payment. PLAYORA bears its provider processing costs; separately charged wallet or exchange sending fees remain your responsibility.

Browsing does not reserve stock. A reservation expires at the time displayed by checkout and may end before a provider quote. Payment confirmation and stock must be reconciled; an expired invoice or late payment cannot take stock already sold. If payment arrived but the promised item cannot be supplied, support arranges a full refund or an alternative you freely accept. We never turn the payment into compulsory credit.

Timed promotions state their end time and timezone, eligible product and quantity, and any real purchase limit. We do not describe ordinary stock as limited without an actual limit. A coupon funded by PLAYORA reduces the paid price; an eligible purchase refund returns that paid amount.

## Recipient details and safe fulfillment

Provide only the necessary game ID, server/region and agreed delivery information. For a product that can be delivered by player ID, do not send a game password. Any genuinely necessary private access must use the protected access flow. PLAYORA never needs your sign-in codes, unrelated email login, wallet private key or seed phrase.

Tell support immediately if a recipient detail is wrong. We can correct an undelivered order when technically possible and after verifying the request. If our team delivers to the wrong recipient or supplies the wrong product, our full-refund protection applies. If you alone supplied incorrect information and we delivered exactly as agreed, we investigate recovery and explain the evidence; no unannounced recovery charge applies.

We do not substitute another item, platform, region or account because it is convenient. A change requires your informed agreement before fulfillment.

## Timing and evidence

The delivery range begins after verified payment and all required recipient details are correct. It is an estimate unless expressly guaranteed. An agreed later appointment begins at that appointment. We explain missing details, outages and pauses rather than marking unfinished delivery complete.

Staff record appropriate delivery evidence, such as the recipient, item, quantity and time, with private details protected. You can review progress and ask questions in the order. A provider payment receipt is not proof that the in-game item was delivered.

## Cancellation, refunds and damage

You can cancel for a full refund before actual fulfillment begins. After correctly performed fulfillment has begun, a change-of-mind cancellation follows only a lawful, clearly pre-agreed basis for work actually supplied. Digital delivery does not by itself remove mandatory consumer rights.

If PLAYORA fails an agreed product, quantity, compatibility, access or guaranteed delivery condition, we refund 100% of the affected order's purchase payment. A partial delivery does not reduce that promise. A replacement is optional when a refund is due. If our delivery actions cause verified account damage, the separate monetary compensation policy also applies.

Report missing, incorrect, invalid or unusable items promptly in the order's Help conversation or at support@playoragames.com. Preserve relevant screenshots and notices without exposing secrets. The standard in-app dispute window is 365 days after completion, but a closed interface window does not remove accepted protections or applicable legal rights. Refund processing, verified destinations and appeals follow the Refunds and Complaints policies.

## Game rules

PLAYORA is independent of game publishers. A publisher may restrict third-party sales or revoke improperly supplied content. We do not promise exemption from those rules. That disclosure does not excuse our failure to provide the accepted product or cancel a protected refund claim.

For help, open the relevant game's Shop service and available FAQs. Support and shop fulfillment operate 24/7, subject to the order's actual delivery conditions.
