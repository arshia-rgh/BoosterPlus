# PLAYORA privacy notice

**Draft for owner review — 13 October 2026. Not published or effective.**

## Who handles your information

The PLAYORA team operates PLAYORA's worldwide game services. Contact support@playoragames.com or your private support conversation for questions or privacy requests. Support is available 24/7; a substantive investigation may take longer than an initial reply.

This notice covers customers, account sellers and people using PLAYORA's public site and account portals. Staff and booster accounts are independent identities, with separate profiles, sessions, permissions and security settings, even when the same email is used. Information is accessed across those boundaries only as needed for authorized work, not because the emails match.

| Relevant request, network, device/browser and operational/security records | Run the service, prevent abuse and investigate faults |

Information comes from you, your use of PLAYORA, your chosen sign-in/payment providers, and authorized staff performing the service. A buyer may supply information about a transferred account and a seller's claim; we use it for that transaction, not as permission to expose their private conversations.

PLAYORA uses Google or email sign-in and has no customer password or stored-value wallet. Google sign-in requests identity and email claims, not access to your Gmail messages. Never submit unrelated email passwords, wallet private keys, seed phrases or unnecessary identity documents through ordinary order chat.

## Purposes and choices

We process necessary account and transaction information to take steps you request and perform your agreements. Security, fraud prevention, service administration and claim defense rely on proportionate legitimate interests where recognized by applicable law. We use a legal-obligation basis only where an actual applicable requirement exists. Optional processing that requires consent has a separate choice; agreeing to general terms is not blanket marketing consent.

Required order information is necessary to provide that service. Optional alerts and reviews are separate, and Google sign-in is optional when email sign-in is available. Withdrawing optional consent does not undo earlier lawful processing or stop necessary transaction/security notices.

Rules calculate estimates and check permissions, availability and payment state. Staff review final custom quotes and contested refund/account decisions. Ask support for an explanation or human review of an incorrect result.

## Who receives information

Authorized staff and assigned boosters receive only the information needed for their task. Private access is restricted and audited. Seller and buyer private messages are not automatically visible to one another. Permitted public listing details and reviews are different from private proof and credentials.

Infrastructure providers support hosting, DNS, network protection and the operation of self-hosted database, private storage and email services. Google authenticates people who select it. NOWPayments processes crypto transactions and its own verification and compliance checks. Your email provider receives messages addressed to you. Social platforms receive information you choose to send through their contact links. Public blockchains may independently expose addresses, amounts and transaction history.

Google and NOWPayments operate under their own privacy notices, available through their services. Provider reviews may require additional information; PLAYORA cannot promise anonymity or that a third party will never request identity verification.

We do not sell personal information or share it for targeted advertising. Private chat, proof and game access are not advertising permission. Separate permission is required for marketing use of private customer material. Proportionate disclosures may be necessary for an applicable legal obligation or a genuine claim; access must be limited to that purpose.

## International processing

Customers, authorized personnel and service providers may be in different countries, so information may be processed outside your country. Worldwide service does not imply that all data remains in one place. Applicable transfer requirements and safeguards must be respected. Contact support for the recipients, locations and transfer arrangements relevant to your information; we do not claim an unverified adequacy decision or certification.

## Storage and retention

We retain information only while it serves an identified transaction, security, claim or applicable legal purpose. The retention schedule distinguishes ordinary messages from essential financial and dispute evidence. Longer retention requires a recorded reason affecting the particular records, not an indefinite hold on everything.

The proposed operational schedule for this version is: ordinary closed support material up to 365 days after the last substantive interaction; closed dispute evidence reviewed at 730 days after closure; routine security/audit records up to 365 days; and limited commercial/payment evidence reviewed at 730 days after settlement. A documented applicable accounting, tax or claim period can require different treatment. These proposed periods must be approved and implemented before this draft takes effect; they are not assertions of automatic deletion already running or of a universal legal minimum.

Eligible profile information should be removed or anonymized within 30 days of a verified closure request, subject to specific lawful holds and any shorter legal deadline. Necessary credentials must be removed as soon as authorized access is no longer needed, with a proposed maximum of 30 days after completed handover/fulfillment absent a specific justified hold. Other temporary data follows its actual technical expiry and cleanup process. Public review attribution is assessed separately from retained financial evidence.

Automatic customer deletion is not enabled. You can still submit a rights request through support. A pending self-service feature does not remove your rights. We distinguish expired access, restricted retained evidence, anonymization and actual file deletion; one is not a promise that every copy has disappeared.

The deployment does not currently have a qualified off-host backup/recovery system. Before one is introduced, copy retention, expiry, restricted restoration and reapplication of deletion decisions must be documented. PLAYORA cannot erase independently maintained public blockchain records.

## Cookies and security

PLAYORA uses first-party sign-in and browser-binding cookies and limited session storage for safe return navigation and non-secret form selections. The Cookies notice describes their purposes and lifetimes. The site follows your device's reduced-motion setting; there is no active site preference that needs a stored motion choice.

The reviewed application has no identified advertising or behavioral-analytics integration. If optional tracking is introduced, we explain it and obtain any required choice before activation. Selecting a third-party sign-in, payment or social link can involve that provider's own storage.

We use encrypted transport, role-based permissions, required staff second factors and protected game-access storage. These safeguards reduce risk; they do not guarantee that information cannot be lost or misused. Report suspected unauthorized access promptly without putting secrets in the report.

## Your rights

You may request access, correction, deletion, restriction or portability and object to processing where applicable law provides those rights. **You can object to processing based on legitimate interests and withdraw optional consent.** We may need proportionate confirmation of account control, using secure channels, rather than unnecessary identity documents in chat.

We aim to respond within one calendar month and respect any shorter applicable deadline. If a permitted extension is necessary, we explain its reason and new deadline. We identify any records that cannot yet be erased and why, rather than describing partial deletion as total erasure. Exports contain authorized records; store them privately.

You may complain to a competent data-protection authority under the law that applies to you. Our complaint process does not remove that right. PLAYORA is intended for people aged 18 or older who can enter a binding agreement; contact us about suspected underage use. This is not a claim that document-based age verification is already implemented.
