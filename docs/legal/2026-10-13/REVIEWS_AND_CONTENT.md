# PLAYORA reviews and content policy

**Draft for owner review — 13 October 2026. Not published or effective.**

## Reviews based on real experience

Reviews must describe a real PLAYORA transaction or direct service experience. Where the review form requires an eligible order, use that order. A review badge describes only the connection we actually checked; it does not certify every statement. Do not submit fabricated reviews, impersonate another customer or arrange fake transactions to affect a rating.

Positive and negative feedback follow the same rules. A low score, refund request, unresolved complaint or criticism of PLAYORA is not a reason to suppress an otherwise valid review. We do not make a refund, payout or support response conditional on a positive review, removal of criticism or silence.

Reviews are optional. We do not buy positive reviews. Any future incentive for honest feedback must be disclosed and must not depend on the score. Staff or people with a commercial relationship must disclose that relationship and must not pose as ordinary buyers.

## What may be published

A published review may show its rating, written feedback, game/service, date and the public attribution explained by the review form. We do not publish account login credentials, payment destinations, private order conversations, email addresses, recovery material or private evidence. Do not include another person's personal information in a review.

By submitting a review for publication, you permit PLAYORA to display and format it as part of the site's review service. This is not ownership of your content or automatic permission to use private correspondence or your image in advertisements. Promotional reuse of private material requires separate permission.

## Moderation

We may withhold, remove or request correction of content containing exposed secrets, unlawful material, threats, targeted harassment, discriminatory abuse, sexual exploitation, malicious links, spam, impersonation, fabricated transaction evidence or irrelevant advertising. Disagreement and fair criticism are allowed.

We choose the least intrusive appropriate response. Removing a password or unrelated identifying detail should not remove valid criticism. We do not rewrite a rating or change the meaning of feedback to improve our reputation. If an edit affects meaning, we ask the author to correct it or explain why publication cannot proceed.

Where contact is available, we explain the rule relied on and provide a route to request review. A staff reply must be courteous and must not reveal private customer records. A review is not the place to investigate a security incident in public; support handles the underlying issue separately.

Featured testimonials are a curated selection, not a complete set of all feedback. Aggregate statistics must use the stated eligible population consistently. We do not present a selected set as an independent or exhaustive rating.

## Reports and appeals

Contact support@playoragames.com with the review or content URL, the issue and relevant evidence. Never send secret account material in a public report. We prioritize exposed credentials, threats and serious privacy problems, and aim to assess other reports within 72 hours of receiving the necessary information. A moderation appeal follows the Complaints policy, with a seven-calendar-day target and reasons for any delay.

## Listings, uploaded images and staff articles

Supply accurate images and account attributes that you are entitled to share. Public covers and screenshots must not expose private material or imply an endorsement that does not exist. PLAYORA may crop, resize or select an appropriate cover for the stated listing or article without changing material facts. Uploading private evidence is not consent to publish it as a cover.

News, patch notes and guides are written and published by PLAYORA staff. Articles should identify the game, author or staff attribution, publication date, relevant game version and material sources. We distinguish confirmed patch information from opinion or a prediction. Report an error through support; significant corrections should be marked. Old articles may no longer describe the current game and are not additional order guarantees.

Game names and publisher assets remain subject to their owners' rights. A person reporting an intellectual-property issue can send the content URL, the work concerned, their connection to it and a safe contact method. We review a credible report, restrict disputed material where appropriate, and allow a response without publishing private evidence. This process does not claim ownership of third-party artwork or replace applicable legal procedures.
