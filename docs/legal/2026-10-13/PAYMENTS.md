# PLAYORA crypto payment policy

**Draft for owner review — 13 October 2026. Not published or effective.**

## Direct purchases and provider instructions

Orders are priced in USD and paid directly in USDT on BNB Smart Chain (BEP-20). This is the only payment network currently offered. There is no customer wallet or account top-up. Use only the exact asset, network, address, amount and any memo shown by your authenticated checkout. USDT sent on another network does not match this checkout.

The exchange-rate mode is fixed for the provider's valid quote. The local checkout window is 30 minutes, but it does not extend the provider's shorter quote or address validity. Follow the displayed expiry and never send to expired instructions. Availability, minimum amounts and network conditions are checked for the selected route. PLAYORA pays the provider processing fees; wallet or exchange sending fees charged directly to you remain your responsibility. Make sure the full displayed payment amount reaches the checkout destination.

Payment is complete only after provider evidence is verified and allocated to your order. A screenshot or transaction hash alone is not proof of payment. Underpayments, excess or duplicate transfers, late payments and wrong-route payments require review. Do not send additional funds without fresh authenticated instructions. Provider or network delays never simulate a successful payment, and a late transfer cannot reserve stock already sold. Contact support if anything is unclear; never share a seed phrase or private key.

The accepted package or order defines every rank, win-rate, hero, role, quantity, delivery and other service condition. If PLAYORA fails an agreed deliverable or condition, you are entitled to a 100% refund of the affected order's purchase amount; partial work does not reduce it. A repeat service, replacement or extension is optional when a refund is due. If an account is reclaimed, restricted, unusable or lacks promised access and review confirms the buyer did not cause the issue, the same full-refund protection applies. Completion alone does not end account protection. Verified account damage caused by our team is assessed for fair monetary compensation separately from the order refund, without automatically capping it at the order price or making an undisputed refund depend on agreement about compensation.

For completed boosting and shop orders, the standard in-app dispute window is 365 days after completion. Account listings retain their separately accepted in-app reporting window. An in-app appeal may be submitted within 30 days of a decision, up to 3 appeals. These interface limits do not remove the refund, account-protection or compensation rights in your accepted terms or any applicable legal rights. If an in-app option is unavailable or its window has ended, contact your order's support conversation or support@playoragames.com for staff review. Existing accepted orders retain their own terms. Support is available 24/7.

## Account sale fees

For new account-sale agreements, the buyer pays the base price plus a 5% commission. The seller receives the agreed base minus a 10% commission. These fees use the same base independently, with each USD fee rounded to the nearest cent, half upward. For a $100 base, buyer total is $105 and seller net is $90. The buyer’s fee is included in a protected full refund. Existing accepted contracts are not repriced. PLAYORA-funded discounts do not reduce accepted seller proceeds. These commissions do not apply to boosting or shop orders. The Account Buying and Selling policy explains different buy-first resale prices, payout eligibility and exact accepted crypto amounts.

## Cancellation

You can cancel for a full refund before fulfillment starts. If you change your mind after correctly performed work starts, only a properly disclosed, lawful, pre-agreed charge for work actually supplied may apply. Without that agreed basis, PLAYORA does not invent a deduction. Cancellation rules never reduce a full refund owed for an unfulfilled agreement, protected account problem or applicable legal right.

## Partial work

If PLAYORA fails an agreed deliverable or condition, the affected order receives a 100% refund even if part of the work was completed. We do not deduct partial work, administration or original processing costs. A repair, repeat service, replacement or extension is optional for the customer when a full refund is due.

## Attendance and pauses

If our team fails an agreed attendance or guaranteed scheduling condition, the full-refund promise applies. If required customer access or availability is missing, we explain the issue and agree how to proceed; we do not silently complete the order or invent a no-show fee. An estimate is not a guaranteed deadline, and a change to an actual guarantee requires customer agreement.

## Unmet conditions

If we miss an accepted rank, win-rate, hero/role restriction, item, quantity, guaranteed deadline or other agreed condition, we refund 100% of the affected order purchase amount. Conditions are defined per package or accepted order, not by a global win-rate target. For account reclamation, bans, restrictions or missing promised access, a full refund applies when review confirms the buyer did not cause the problem. Verified damage caused by our team is reviewed for separate fair monetary compensation under the accepted terms.

## Refund amount and destination

A full refund returns 100% of the purchase payment allocated to the affected order, including charges collected by PLAYORA for it. By default we return the original crypto asset and original number of units allocated to the order. A different supported asset/network or valuation requires the customer's express agreement to the exact amount first. Previous partial refunds count toward that same order total. Excess and duplicate payments are accounted for separately and never become a site balance. Direct third-party wallet/exchange costs and later market-value changes are not part of the order purchase amount; applicable additional rights remain. Confirm a safe recipient asset/network/address with authenticated support; an exchange sending address is not automatically a refund destination.

## Refund and compensation costs

PLAYORA pays the outgoing refund fee so it does not reduce an eligible full refund. We do not deduct administration or our original processing costs. PLAYORA also covers outgoing fees on agreed account-damage compensation. Separately charged wallet/exchange sending fees paid directly to third parties are not included in the order purchase amount, without restricting any further remedy required by law. We aim to assess a claim within 72 hours of necessary evidence and initiate an approved refund within 48 hours of confirming a usable destination, respecting any shorter legal deadline. Provider checks or complex reviews receive an explanation and updates at least every 48 hours while action is pending. Completion requires verified transfer evidence.

## Seller payouts

Seller offers separately state the accepted net amount and payment event. Consignment ordinarily uses a seven-day buyer inspection period followed by a 48-hour initiation target after eligibility and destination verification. Buy-first uses a 48-hour target after accepted handover/control and destination verification, before resale. PLAYORA pays outgoing costs so the accepted net is not reduced. An unpaid obligation is not a wallet balance. Provider checks, missing prerequisites and specific disputes receive explained review; none creates an unannounced additional fee.

## Verification and support

Only the authenticated checkout and verified transfer workflow provide payment instructions. Provider completion is verified before fulfillment; a browser return, email, screenshot or transaction hash alone cannot mark an order paid. Contact support@playoragames.com for a delayed or inconsistent status. Do not make a second payment merely because the order screen has not updated. We reconcile funds and communicate the next step.
