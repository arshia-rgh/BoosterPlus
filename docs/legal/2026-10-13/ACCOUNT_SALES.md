# PLAYORA account buying and selling policy

**Draft for owner review — 13 October 2026. Not published or effective.**

## Account prices and commission

The account base price is the price before PLAYORA's account-sale commission. The buyer pays a commission of **5% of the base price in addition to that price**. The seller pays a commission of **10% of the agreed base price**, deducted from the seller's proceeds. These percentages apply to account sales only, not boosting or shop orders.

For a $100 base price, the buyer pays $105, the seller receives $90, and PLAYORA's combined commission is $15 before its costs. The seller does not pay a separate advance fee to submit an account, receive an offer, or unlock a payout.

Each commission is calculated independently on the base price and rounded to the nearest USD cent, with an exact half cent rounded upward. The buyer total is the base plus the rounded buyer fee; seller net is the base minus the rounded seller fee. We never calculate the seller fee on the buyer's fee-inclusive total. The exact breakdown is shown before acceptance and payment. Required taxes, if applicable, must be accounted for and disclosed within the confirmed amounts; we do not add an undisclosed tax or administrative charge later.

A seller-approved change to the base price requires a replacement agreement before it affects the seller's proceeds. A discount funded entirely by PLAYORA reduces the buyer's payable amount without reducing the seller's accepted net proceeds. A protected refund returns the amount actually paid, including the buyer commission, not an undiscounted price the buyer never paid.

These fees apply only to new agreements accepted after this policy takes effect. Existing accepted agreements keep their agreed charges.

## Submitting an account

Submit the actual game, region/server, rank, assets, skin types/counts, linked access, restrictions and history. Images must be accurate and must not expose secrets or unrelated private details. Staff may request ownership evidence and select an authorized public cover.

You must control the account and have authority to transfer it. Do not submit stolen, borrowed, disputed, concurrently sold or fraudulent accounts. Disclose bans, warnings, recovery claims, debts, chargebacks, cooldowns and untransferable access. Submission approval is not a sale or payout agreement.

A separate offer specifies the arrangement, base price, 10% fee, exact net crypto amount/network, transfer requirements, payout timing and expiry. You may decline. Unaccepted offers expire after seven calendar days unless a shorter expiry is shown. Accepted offers are not silently repriced.

## Selling through PLAYORA after resale

For consignment, we arrange resale after verifying account control. Seller payment depends on a buyer paying and receiving the account. There is no guaranteed sale date or payout merely because an unsold listing has aged.

The standard initial inspection period is **seven calendar days after the buyer acknowledges delivery**. The seller payout becomes eligible once that period ends, verified buyer funds are allocated, the order is completed, and relevant open delivery, refund or dispute issues are resolved. An acknowledgement alone does not prove that every protection claim has ended.

Once eligible, with a verified payout destination, PLAYORA aims to initiate payment within **48 hours**, including weekends. We do not impose an additional routine waiting period after the inspection period. Provider confirmation can take longer; we supply verified transfer evidence rather than treating a submitted transfer as received.

If acknowledgement is missing, we contact the buyer, check handover and explain the delay; we do not fabricate it. A dispute hold identifies the transaction and reason, receives updates at least every 48 hours and review at least every seven days. It is not indefinite confiscation.

The seven-day inspection period controls the ordinary consignment payout timetable. It is **not** an expiry of the buyer's account protection. A later protected buyer claim is handled separately and is not automatically charged to an innocent seller.

## Selling to PLAYORA before resale

For buy-first, PLAYORA purchases before resale. The agreed base purchase price minus the 10% seller commission gives your net purchase payment.

After you accept and we verify the required account control, with a verified payout destination, we aim to initiate that net payment within **48 hours**. The account is not published for resale until your purchase payout is confirmed. An eventual resale, a later buyer's inspection period or PLAYORA's resale profit is not a condition for paying this purchase obligation.

If PLAYORA later changes its resale base price, the next buyer's 5% fee is calculated on that later price. Your already-agreed purchase price does not change. The combined 15% illustration above applies when the buyer and seller use the same base price; a buy-first transaction with a different later resale price has a different commercial margin.

## Crypto amount and destination

The initial payout route is **USDT on BNB Smart Chain, BEP-20**; other USDT networks are not interchangeable. Your offer shows net units, USD base/fees, conversion rate/source and timestamp before acceptance. USDT is not assumed always to equal one USD. The accepted crypto amount is fixed.

PLAYORA pays the outgoing payout network and provider costs so they do not reduce the accepted net amount. There is no additional PLAYORA withdrawal fee, wallet balance or site top-up. If a provider minimum would prevent this exact payout, we resolve it before accepting the offer, instead of withholding a small balance indefinitely.

Confirm the destination and any required memo through the authenticated transaction flow. Do not send a seed phrase, private key or wallet login. Address changes require verification before sending. We cannot reverse a confirmed transfer to a wrong destination you expressly confirmed, but we investigate available recovery options. PLAYORA remains responsible for an address or amount error made by its team. Provider checks and lawful restrictions may delay a transfer; we communicate the reason we can share and the next review time.

## Withdrawing a sale

You can withdraw an unaccepted submission or decline an offer without a commission. After accepting a consignment offer, request withdrawal through your submission conversation. If the account is not reserved, sold or under a specific dispute hold, we unpublish it and arrange secure return of control, aiming to complete this within 48 hours. We charge no routine listing-removal fee.

If a buyer reservation or paid order exists, we explain its status and resolve it before returning control. Do not recover the account yourself while another person may have paid for it. A failed reservation must be reconciled before release. After a completed buy-first purchase, you cannot unilaterally take the account back; a reversal needs written agreement and coordinated return of both account and payment.

If a sale is cancelled before completion and no seller breach is established, no successful-sale commission is earned on that cancelled sale. For consignment, there are no sale proceeds to pay until a valid sale completes. A new sale or changed offer requires clear acceptance, not a hidden carry-over charge.

## Buyer delivery and protection

Read the listing’s game, region/server, attributes, included access, restrictions, transfer method and delivery range before paying. After verified payment we coordinate private handover. Perform agreed security changes, report missing access, and acknowledge delivery only after checking it.

**If the account is reclaimed, banned, restricted, unusable, materially misdescribed or missing promised access, and review confirms the buyer did not cause the problem, PLAYORA refunds 100% of the amount paid for the account order, including the 5% buyer commission.** This includes previous-owner misconduct, undisclosed restrictions and a restriction caused by the sale or transfer itself when the buyer did not independently cause it.

Ordinary play, contacting support and following agreed security instructions do not cancel protection. An allegation of cheating, unauthorized sharing or another buyer action must be connected by evidence to the particular problem; we do not presume fault because an issue appeared later. Completing the order, the seven-day inspection period, or a seller payout does not end this promise. If the order's report button is unavailable, contact support@playoragames.com.

Publisher rules may prohibit account transfers. PLAYORA is not a publisher and cannot guarantee permanent access or immunity from enforcement. This disclosure does not override the protection just promised. A replacement account or restoration may be offered, but cannot be forced instead of an eligible refund.

## Disputes involving sellers

We review seller, buyer and PLAYORA evidence separately, preserve private conversations, and give the seller a chance to respond to an allegation. A buyer refund is not delayed until PLAYORA recovers money from a seller.

If a cancellation is caused by the buyer or PLAYORA and the seller fulfilled the accepted agreement, we do not automatically penalize the seller or recover an already-earned payout. If evidence establishes seller fraud, reclamation, false material information or another seller breach causing the loss, we may seek return of the attributable net payout or evidenced direct loss through a written settlement or lawful recovery process. We do not impose an automatic penalty, charge the buyer's 5% fee to the seller, collect twice for the same loss, or seize unrelated proceeds without a lawful basis. Any offset must be lawful, explained and supported by the accepted agreement or subsequent consent.

Request a second review through the submission conversation or support email. Our complaint process applies to both parties. Mandatory legal rights take priority over conflicting wording.
