## What to send

Tell us the game, service, order or submission reference if you have one, what you need, and any relevant time or error message. For access loss, an unexpected payment or a suspected security problem, say so at the start of the message. Remove unrelated personal details from screenshots.

Never send PLAYORA sign-in codes, authenticator or recovery codes, unrelated email passwords, wallet private keys or seed phrases. Use the protected access screen for game credentials. Staff will not ask you to pay a private social-media contact to unlock a refund or seller payout.

## Response and escalation

We respond around the clock. A reply does not always mean a payment, investigation or order can finish immediately. We aim to provide substantive refund and complaint assessments within 72 hours of necessary evidence, updates at least every 48 hours on pending cases, and an appeal outcome within seven calendar days. Provider and blockchain confirmations may take additional time. Shorter applicable legal deadlines take priority.

For a privacy request, say which data or account you mean; we aim to respond within one calendar month, subject to any shorter applicable deadline. If you disagree with a decision, request a second review in the same conversation or by email.

Game pages contain their available service details, FAQs, news and guides. The accepted order remains the reference for your individual promises; general help cannot silently reduce them.
