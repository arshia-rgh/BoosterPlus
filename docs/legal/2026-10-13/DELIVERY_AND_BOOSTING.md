# PLAYORA delivery and boosting policy

**Draft for owner review — 13 October 2026. Not published or effective.**

## How an order is agreed

PLAYORA offers services worldwide and operates support and fulfillment 24/7. Product availability still depends on the game, platform, region/server, stock, staff availability and supported payment route. Availability around the clock does not mean instant fulfillment.

Fixed packages have an administrator-defined scope, price and conditions. You choose whether to buy that package; required recipient or access details do not make it a custom quote. A custom-boost form collects your requested result and provides an estimate. Staff issue the final offer, and you accept its exact scope and price before payment. A requested option that has not been accepted is not a promise.

The accepted order records the game, region/server, current and target state, play mode, any hero/role restrictions, win-rate conditions, delivery range, whether timing is estimated or guaranteed, start conditions and completion evidence. Later catalog edits do not replace that agreement.

## Delivery clocks and progress

The delivery clock starts when payment is verified and all agreed prerequisites are ready: correct account/recipient information, required secure access and, for playing together, the agreed available session. A reserved later appointment starts at that appointment. We identify a missing prerequisite and tell you how to resolve it; an unexplained internal queue does not silently move a guaranteed deadline.

Unless expressly stated otherwise in the accepted offer, a delivery range is an estimate in elapsed hours from that start event. A guaranteed upper bound is stated explicitly. Any proposed guaranteed-deadline change needs your agreement. We do not replace it with an estimated date after payment.

We record progress and delivery evidence in your order and aim to provide an update at least every 24 hours while active, and whenever a significant problem affects the plan. A timestamp shows when information was last updated. You can contact support at any time.

We explain pauses caused by missing access, your requested schedule, maintenance, a security concern or an investigation. Any effect on guaranteed timing must follow the accepted order or be agreed with you. A pause is not completion. If we cannot meet an agreed condition, the full-refund policy applies; further work or an extension is your choice.

## Playing on your account

Only authorized staff and the assigned booster may use necessary game access for the accepted task. Share it through the protected access screen, never public uploads or ordinary chat. We do not need your PLAYORA codes, unrelated personal email password, payment login, wallet private key or seed phrase.

Our team must not use cheats or bots, make unauthorized purchases, sell or remove assets, change recovery ownership, or access unrelated communications. Necessary in-game actions must follow the agreed order. Coordinate your own login with the team to avoid conflicting sessions. Change temporary game access after completion or when support recommends it. Report unexpected access immediately.

## Playing together

You play your own account and join the agreed session, region/server and mode. No game password is needed for ordinary party play. Confirm compatible access and availability before a timed appointment. If either side misses a session, we record the issue and agree rescheduling or the appropriate refund; no hidden no-show charge applies.

## Win rate and match evidence

There is no universal minimum win rate. The final package or custom quote must state whether a WR condition is guaranteed and exactly what it measures. A preference alone is not a purchased guarantee. The current standard fixed rank packages do not include a WR target unless their accepted description expressly says so.

The default measurement for a separately accepted order-match WR guarantee is the number of won matches divided by the number of completed counted matches, multiplied by 100. The quote identifies the account, queue/mode, start/end points, and counted matches. Overall historical account WR and a particular hero's lifetime WR are different metrics and are not substituted for order-match WR. If the desired metric is historical WR, staff must expressly quote that metric and its starting data.

| Accepted preference | Meaning when expressly guaranteed |
| --- | --- |
| No preference | No numerical WR condition; the other agreed conditions still apply |
| Between X and Y | At least X% and at most Y%, inclusive |
| Below X | Strictly below X% |
| Above Y | Strictly above Y% |
| Exactly 100% | Every counted match must be a win |

We assess the actual fraction without rounding it into compliance. For example, 7 wins from 10 counted matches is exactly 70%, which meets a 70–80% range but not a strictly-above-70% requirement. A sample of zero matches has no WR and cannot satisfy a WR promise. Minimum match counts and any completion condition must be agreed beforehand.

A completed match played by our team within the agreed scope counts whether won or lost, including a completed match affected by disconnection. A publisher-recorded remake or void result without a completed outcome is excluded and identified. Customer-only matches outside an agreed team session are excluded from an order-match measure and logged separately. We do not selectively remove losses after the fact. A different rule must be explicit before acceptance and must not misrepresent the result.

We do not intentionally lose, manipulate matchmaking or continue extra games without agreement to fit an upper WR bound. If a range or below-threshold request would require that, staff must explain the problem and revise or decline it before acceptance. Once a condition is accepted, it cannot simply be ignored; failure engages the agreed refund protection.

## Heroes, roles and game changes

The accepted quote identifies any mandatory hero or role and permissible alternatives. If an agreed hero is banned, unavailable or unsuitable after a patch, we follow the pre-agreed fallback or ask you before changing the restriction. A form's linked hero/role and region/server choices help selection; they do not authorize staff to replace the final agreement.

Record relevant starting rank, stars, season and game version. If a season reset, patch or publisher action prevents the promised result, we explain the situation. We do not silently substitute the new season's rank or treat a previous estimate as permission to miss a guaranteed condition. A mutually accepted revision or the refund policy resolves the affected order.

## Completion, shop and account handover

Completion evidence must relate to the actual order and agreed result. Review it and report missing conditions. Marking an order complete does not remove an eligible refund or separate account-damage claim.

Shop products are fulfilled manually by PLAYORA staff after verified payment. Their offer states the product, quantity, compatible region/server, recipient and delivery method. No substitution or extra charge is made without agreement. Incorrect, missing or unusable delivery and missed agreed conditions are covered by the full-refund policy; wrong recipient information supplied solely by the customer is investigated on its evidence.

Account listings identify included login/recovery access, transfer restrictions and a delivery range. Staff arrange private handover and security steps. A delivery acknowledgement should be given only after checking the promised access. The standard seven-day account inspection period is separate from the full-refund protection, which does not disappear when that period ends.

## Information shared with staff

Assigned staff receive the order selections, necessary progress and minimum access required to perform their task. Buyer and seller private conversations remain separate. Staff access to protected credentials follows the approved secure workflow. Public listing or review permission is not consent to publish private proofs or use them in advertising.

Game publishers may prohibit boosting or account sharing and may take action against accounts. We are independent of them and do not promise immunity. This does not cancel PLAYORA's agreed full-refund or service-caused damage commitments. Open the game's service page for relevant FAQs and contact support@playoragames.com for order-specific help.
