## How later edits work

1. Edit an unpublished revision and review its effects on actual operations, checkout, help and privacy notices.
2. Publish it with a new immutable version and effective date. Keep previous published texts and acceptance records accessible; never overwrite them in place.
3. New orders record the applicable accepted version and their own package/quote conditions. Existing orders retain their accepted commercial protections; a later edit cannot silently weaken their refund, compensation or other agreed rights.
4. Notify affected users and collect fresh acceptance where required. Any agreed change to an existing order must be explicit and recorded. Necessary privacy processing changes and legally required protections follow their applicable rules, not an assumed retroactive commercial rewrite.
5. Keep backend/frontend bootstrap versions consistent and preserve historical bootstrap text. Published policy revisions use the existing owner-managed content and acceptance workflow. Test stale-consent rejection and historical order access before releasing related code changes.

No payment, payout, email or public activation is performed by recording this approval.
